# Safety and interpretation

Wastewater Watch is deliberately scoped to defensive surveillance.

## Supported

- Quality control and provenance tracking
- Taxonomic community profiling
- Longitudinal anomaly screening
- Antimicrobial-resistance surveillance as a future defensive extension
- Human analyst review and escalation

## Unsupported

- Clinical diagnosis or individual risk prediction
- Declaring that an outbreak has occurred
- Pathogen optimization, design, or enhancement
- Inferring harmful experimental modifications
- Processing data without authorization or appropriate privacy controls

Environmental metagenomes can contain human reads. The real-data workflow makes
host filtering mandatory and does not retain the aligned host reads.
